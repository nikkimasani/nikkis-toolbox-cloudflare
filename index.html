How I build

Every app here started as something I needed. I scope the problem, design the experience, build it with modern AI coding tools, and ship it to a real URL, then keep iterating as I use it.

That loop of defining what matters, cutting scope, and shipping something people can actually use is the same work whether the user is me or a whole team.
